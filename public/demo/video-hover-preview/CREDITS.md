## Interview clips (with sound and captions)

Real interview fragments with chefs, from the YouTube channel [Cooking for the future project](https://www.youtube.com/@Cookingforthefutureproject) (The CORE project, interviews with professionals about the skills a chef of the future needs, 2019). The people speak English in their own voices; nothing is synthesized or generated. License of all three videos: [Creative Commons Attribution license (CC BY 3.0, "reuse allowed" on YouTube)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Author: Cooking for the future project.

| File | Speaker | Source | Cut from source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `louise-brosnan.mp4` | Louise Brosnan, chef, Dingle (Ireland) | [Louise Brosnan, Head Chef, interview 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX40Otmn6is) | 1:24.8 to 1:35.7 |
| `toni-aarniviita.mp4` | Toni Aarniviita, head chef, Finland | [Toni Aarniviita Executive Chef, interview 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p90QXBIpDZo) | 2:06.0 to 2:23.6 |
| `arto-huhtala.mp4` | Arto Huhtala, head chef, Finland | [Arto Huhtala Executive Chef, interview 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=psbvfsaUAU0) | 2:18.0 to 2:35.5 |

Changes made by us (CC BY requires saying so): each clip is trimmed to one answer (the interviewer's question is cut off), re-encoded to H.264 + AAC with `+faststart` at the source resolution (640x360, not upscaled), loudness normalized to about -16 LUFS, and given a short audio fade. The poster (`*.webp`) is a frame from the clip. Captions (`*.en.vtt`, `*.ru.vtt`) come from our own transcription of the English speech (whisper.cpp, cleaned up by hand); the Russian version is a free translation, not word for word. One video file serves both locales, only the captions change.

The same files are used by the site on every locale. The quotes on the cards are phrases the speakers said, no fictional testimonials.
